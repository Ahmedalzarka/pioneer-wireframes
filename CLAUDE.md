# Pioneer Paper Industries (PPI) website concepts

Client: Pioneer Paper Industries (بايونير للصناعات الورقية), Egypt. Grey board 700 to 2000 GSM, 140 cm sheets, multiple densities, for packaging and premium hard covers. Customers: Egyptian and Gulf companies.

Goals: showcase products, register new clients. Must be premium. Bilingual EN / AR (RTL).

Brand colors: navy #14213D (deep #0A1326), silver gray #A7ADB5 / #C9CED6, kraft #C49A6C / #E2C7A2. Fonts: Manrope (EN), Cairo (AR).

Live: https://ahmedalzarka.github.io/pioneer-wireframes/ (GitHub Pages from main, root).

## Files
- index.html: picker page linking all options
- option-1.html, option-2.html, option-3.html: static wireframes (Industrial Luxury, Material Lab, Factory Story)
- option-4.html: main concept "The Roll (cinematic)". Scroll-scrubbed hero video of a kraft roll (camera push-in), thickness section with GSM counter, specs strip, applications, factory loop video, values, trade account form, EN/AR toggle
- logo.jpg: PPI logo cropped from client poster
